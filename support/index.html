Delete your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time:

  1. Open BoomKit and tap your photo at the top right.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Read what the app deletes and what it keeps, then confirm.

No longer have the app? Email us from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We confirm by email when it’s done. The privacy policy lists what we delete and which pay and tax records we keep.
