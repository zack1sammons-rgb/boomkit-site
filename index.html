Get paid to post for brands.

BoomKit is an iPhone app for creators. You join a brand campaign, post short videos on your own TikTok or Instagram, paste each link into the app and get paid by the campaign’s rules.

See the pay and apply Support

How BoomKit works

  • iPhone only

    BoomKit runs on iPhone. Invited creators get it through Apple’s TestFlight first.

  • 18 and older

    You confirm your age when you sign up. The app turns away anyone under 18.

  • Your own accounts

    You post from TikTok or Instagram accounts you own. We never ask for your passwords.

  • Pay you can check

    The app reads each post’s public views and shows what every video has earned.
