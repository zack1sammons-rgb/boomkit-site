Contractor agreement

Version 2026-10-14 · Last updated: October 28, 2026

Independent contractor terms for creators who post for BoomKit campaigns. They sit alongside our Terms of use and Privacy policy.

1. Parties

These terms are between you and BoomKit LLC, a Utah limited liability company at 1136 E 820 N, Provo, UT 84606, which operates BoomKit. Questions: zack@boomkit.app.

2. Status

Creators are independent contractors aged 18 or older. They choose which briefs to accept, make their own videos and post them on their own accounts with the ad disclosure on.

3. Pay rule

Pay comes from each brand campaign's pool: $1.50 per 1K views + $5 a day for posting. Daily pay rises to $6 on a 7-day streak, more by tier. There is no random money anywhere.

Daily pay: $5.00 to $6.00 by streak day (+$0.50 Silver, +$1 Gold, +$2 Diamond), paid once per campaign per day on the first qualifying post. View pay: $1.50 per 1K views up to 100K, $1.00 per 1K up to 1M and $0.50 per 1K after. Bonuses: +$50 at 100K and +$250 at 1M. The first 1,000 views pay $0. The rate starts after the first 1K. Tier boost on view pay: +2% Silver, +4% Gold, +6% Diamond. Views count for 14 days from posting. One video earns at most 25% of a campaign's pool, and one creator at most 35%. View pay lands Mondays. Credits can be cashed out 14 days after they land. At Bronze, 10K views pay $13.50, 100K pay $198.50 and 1M pay $1,348.50.

Cash-outs start at $10. A creator never pays BoomKit anything. If a post is rejected or its views turn out to be bought, what it earned is taken back while the money is still in its 14-day hold, before it can be cashed out, and is never billed.
